\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Mental health challenges such as stress, depression, and emotional instability have become increasingly common in today's fast-paced world. These conditions significantly impact individual well-being, workplace productivity, academic performance, and overall quality of life. Traditional detection methods, including clinical evaluations and self-reported assessments, often suffer from limitations such as subjectivity, delayed diagnosis, and limited accessibility. This creates a growing need for innovative, affordable, and non-invasive solutions for early mental health detection.

This project, titled "AI-Based Multimodal Stress Detection System Using Vocal and Physical Parameters," focuses on developing an intelligent system to identify stress, depression, and emotional states using vocal analysis and multimodal data processing. The system analyzes vocal features such as pitch, tone, speech rate, pauses, and energy patterns along with relevant physical parameters to detect mental health conditions accurately.

The proposed solution uses benchmark datasets such as RAVDESS for emotion recognition and DAIC for depression analysis. Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)-based models are employed to classify emotional states and detect depression levels from vocal characteristics. A locally hosted user-friendly interface enables real-time analysis and prediction, ensuring ease of use and accessibility.

From an entrepreneurship perspective, this project addresses a significant gap in the healthcare and wellness technology sector. The growing demand for digital mental health solutions presents strong opportunities for commercialization in hospitals, educational institutions, corporate wellness programs, and personal healthcare applications. Additionally, this project offers valuable Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) opportunities through patents, copyrights, trademarks, and proprietary algorithms. Overall, the project demonstrates the potential of Artificial Intelligence in transforming mental healthcare by delivering an innovative, scalable, and accessible solution for early stress and depression detection.
