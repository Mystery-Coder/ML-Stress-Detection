\chapter{Results}

We achieved notable results in predicting depression levels and emotions. For depression prediction, the model attained an overall accuracy of 78\%, with a macro average precision of 82\%, recall of 78\%, and an F1-score of 77\%. Class-wise performance indicates that Class 0 achieved a precision of 82\%, recall of 56\%, and F1-score of 67\%; Class 1 achieved 64\%, 100\%, and 78\%, respectively; and Class 2 achieved 100\%, 77\%, and 87\%.

For emotion prediction, the overall accuracy was 77\%, with a macro average precision, recall, and F1-score of 77\% each. Individual emotion classes showed consistent performance, with precision values ranging from 69\% (neutral) to 83\% (disgust), and F1-scores ranging from 75\% (disgust) to 84\% (surprised). These results demonstrate the model's robustness in identifying both depression levels and emotional states effectively.

The confusion matrix provides a detailed breakdown of the model's performance by showing the counts of true positives, true negatives, false positives, and false negatives for each class. It helps in understanding the types of errors the model makes, such as misclassifications between specific classes. By analyzing the confusion matrix, we can identify patterns in the model's predictions and focus on improving underperforming classes.
